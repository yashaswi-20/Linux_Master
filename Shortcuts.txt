
Commenting Multiple Lines at Once

• Start selection. Move your cursor to the first line you want to comment and press Alt+A (or Ctrl+6) to set a mark.
• Highlight lines. Use the Up or Down arrow keys to highlight all the lines you want to include.
• Apply comment. Press Alt+3 (or Esc then 3) to comment or uncomment the entire selected block.
